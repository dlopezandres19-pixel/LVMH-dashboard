📊 Data Sources & Methodology
①
WeChat Index: Weighted engagement score from 1.38B monthly active users. Measures public content volume + engagement. Primary signal for mainland consumer sentiment.
②
Google Trends: Relative popularity 0–100. China data = VPN users (~3%) — directional only. Global data fully representative.
③
NBS Data: Official China government statistics. Monthly retail YoY%. Published ~15th at stats.gov.cn/english.
④
Resale (Rebag US / Vestiaire EU / Xianyu CN): VR = median resale ÷ MSRP. US from Rebag (authenticated). EU from Vestiaire Collective (authenticated). China from Xianyu (unauthenticated P2P) — filtered for replicas; treat as directional. China VR below 100% on Hermès may reflect daigou (代购) — parallel importers sourcing in Europe and underselling the local MSRP, which is inflated by import duties.
⑤
Disclaimer: Research tool only — not investment advice. Always combine with LVMH/peer earnings and professional financial analysis.
